## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create a clean horizontal adaptation of the supplied company logo for a website sidebar. Input image is the brand identity reference: preserve its distinctive stylized B emblem and bold condensed uppercase lettering. Landscape canvas approximately 3:1. Place the emblem on the left, 'IZAIR BORBA' to its right, and '& CIA LTDA' in a smaller second line below the company name. Exact text: 'IZAIR BORBA' and '& CIA LTDA'. All emblem and lettering are solid clean white, no texture, no scratches, no distress, no grain, no glow, no shadows, crisp flat vector-like edges. Background is solid uniform dark navy #0f172a, edge to edge, without gradient or texture. Frame tightly with a small margin so that the artwork uses almost the full width and is readable when displayed at 192 pixels wide. Do not include 'Comercial 2026', a tagline, a mockup, or additional objects.
